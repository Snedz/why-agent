## The one requirement that decides the entry

> Entrants must create a working software application that **runs on either
> Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud and uses at least one NVIDIA open
> source model**, and that fits into one of the four hackathon tracks.

> "Runs on Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud" means the project **makes a
> runtime call to the Token Factory inference API**, or is deployed/run using
> Nebius AI Cloud compute (Serverless Jobs, Serverless Endpoints, or DevPods).

The Best Apps and Agents track says: *"Power it with Nemotron models on Nebius
through Token Factory. Reach for Nemotron 3 Ultra when you need serious
reasoning, and let Nano or Super handle the fast, everyday calls."* — which is
the shape of this build.

## Tracks

1. **Coding and Agentic Engineering** — agents that write, run and test code.
2. **Best Apps and Agents** — any app or agent someone would actually use.
3. **Personal AI** — an always-on, private assistant.
4. **Physical AI** — embodied and edge agents; the video must show the hardware
   operating, or the key modules in action.

## Submission requirements — the checklist this entry answers

1. A Project built with the required tools, meeting the requirements above.
2. A URL to a working demo, hosted app, or test build.
3. A text description of the features and functionality.
4. A URL to the public code repository (GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket), containing
   all source, assets and instructions; **public**, with an open source licence
   file (Apache 2.0, MIT or MPL 2.0) detectable and visible in the repository's
   About section.
5. A README with setup instructions and clear guidance for running it.
6. **Highlight how you used NVIDIA Nemotron or other NVIDIA open source models,
   where Token Factory accelerated your workflow, and any other Nebius tools or
   services used.**
7. A demonstration video: **under three minutes**; footage showing the project
   functioning on the device for which it was built; **uploaded to and made
   publicly visible on YouTube**, with the link on the form; no third-party
   trademarks or copyrighted material without permission.
8. Identify the track.
9. **Provide feedback on Nebius Token Factory, AI Cloud and any NVIDIA tools,
   models or technologies used.**
10. If the project existed before the Submission Period, a written explanation
    of what was significantly updated during it.

## Testing and availability

Access must be provided for judging and testing. The project must be available
**free of charge and without any restriction** for testing, evaluation and use
by the Sponsor, Administrator and Judges **until the Judging Period ends
(2026-12-23)**.

## Judging

**Stage one** is pass/fail on baseline viability: does the project reasonably
fit the theme and reasonably apply the required APIs/SDKs? A Project fits if it
is a genuine attempt at the track's stated goal, not a superficial rebrand.

**Stage two** scores four equally weighted criteria:

| Criterion | The question |
|---|---|
| Technological Implementation | how well is it built, and how effectively does it use Token Factory or AI Cloud models and NVIDIA Nemotron or other NVIDIA open source models |
| Design | a complete, coherent product experience — not just a technical proof of concept |
| Potential Impact | a credible, specific case for solving a real problem for a real audience, actually addressed by what is demonstrated |
| Quality of the Idea | a creative, non-obvious use of Token Factory or AI Cloud models and NVIDIA open source models, with genuine understanding of the problem space |

Ties break on the first applicable criterion.

## Eligibility — the exclusions to check first

**Open to:** individuals of the age of majority; teams; and **organizations**
(corporations, non-profits, LLCs, partnerships) that exist and are organised or
incorporated at the time of entry. If an organization enters, it must appoint
one authorized Representative.

**Not open to:** residents of, or organizations domiciled in, a jurisdiction
where participating or receiving a prize is prohibited — the rules name
**Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea**, and anything
comprehensively sanctioned by OFAC; organizations and people involved with
running or promoting the hackathon (the Sponsor and Administrator, and their
employees, agents, family and household); any Judge or their employer; parent
companies, subsidiaries and affiliates of the above; and anyone whose
participation would create a real or apparent conflict of interest.

## Financial or preferential support — read this one

> A Project must not have been developed, or derived from a Project developed,
> with financial or preferential support from the Sponsor or Administrator. Such
> Projects include, but are not limited to, those that received funding or
> investment for their development, were developed under contract, or received a
> commercial license, from the Sponsor or Administrator any time prior to the end
> of Hackathon Submission Period.

The Sponsor is Nebius B.V. The rules separately say entrants "will obtain access
to the required developer tools/platform", and the Nebius Builder Program gives
credits for Token Factory, Tavily and Nebius Academy. Free contest credits
handed to every entrant are not funding, a contract, or a commercial licence —
but this entry must hold that line: **no paid Nebius account, no commercial
licence, no contract, no investment.** Contest infrastructure for the prize
window only.

## Other rules that bind the entry

- **Language:** all submission materials in English, or with an English
  translation.
- **Third-party integrations:** any third-party SDK, API or data used must be
  authorised under its terms. (Tavily and the synthetic fixtures in this
  repository are the two third parties here.)
- **IP:** the submission must be the entrant's original work, solely owned, and
  must not infringe third-party rights. Open-source components are allowed
  provided the licences are complied with and the submission builds on them.
  The video must not include third-party trademarks or copyrighted music
  without permission.
- **Multiple submissions:** an entrant may submit more than one, but each must
  be unique and substantially different.
- **Drafts** can be saved until the Submission Period ends.
